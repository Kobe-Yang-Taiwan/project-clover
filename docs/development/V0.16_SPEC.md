# Project Clover V0.16 — Import Correctness & Reconstruction

Status: APPROVED
Product owner: Founder
Execution model: GitHub-driven via repository `AGENTS.md`
Target branch: `agent/flutter-prototype-v0`
Draft PR: #1
Do not merge `main`.

## 1. Mission
V0.16 must make Universal Import materially trustworthy and materially reduce review burden.

Primary product objective:
- reconstruct one real product into exactly one candidate;
- prevent non-products from becoming candidates;
- prevent cross-product / cross-cell contamination;
- preserve correct merchant / brand / product / price / date relationships;
- never fabricate uncertain data.

Do NOT optimize for the largest number of candidates. Optimize for correct, useful candidates and safe uncertainty handling.

The Founder’s desired end state is correct coupon information with fewer manual steps, increasing the chance that users will actually use Project Clover regularly. A 100% target means 100% correctness of records that pass the final persistence gate; it is NOT permission to claim universal OCR recognition accuracy. If data is uncertain, surface it for review or omit it rather than inventing it.

## 2. Evidence / observed V0.15 failures
Founder Android tests showed meaningful improvement but remaining errors in both supermarket multi-product screenshots and Costco PDF imports.

Observed failure classes include:
- non-product text becoming candidates;
- campaign titles, disclaimers, labels, standalone prices or store text becoming product names;
- one product split into multiple candidates;
- neighboring products merged or fields transferred across cells;
- brand, merchant, product name, model, specification and price meanings mixed;
- pure price fragments used as names or sources;
- original price inferred where the source did not explicitly support it;
- savings shown without reliable compatible original/promotional prices;
- product name incomplete or polluted by ITEM/price/promotion text;
- low-confidence candidates still creating excessive review work;
- page/cell structure lost after OCR despite text having been recognized.

Representative previously reported bad patterns include names such as campaign/store disclaimers, `售價以官網為準`, `線上購物亦有優惠`, `活動數量有限｜售完為止`, `【賣場售價】`, standalone currency/price strings, and supermarket campaign copy such as full-spend reward messages. These are evidence for rejection rules, not an exhaustive hard-coded blacklist.

## 3. P0 priorities
### P0.1 Non-product rejection before candidate creation
Create/strengthen a product-likeness gate before a candidate can enter review.

Reject or route out content that is only:
- status/navigation UI;
- campaign headings;
- store/page headers or footers;
- legal/disclaimer text;
- standalone ITEM/SKU identifiers;
- standalone prices/discount fragments;
- generic sale labels;
- online-shopping notices;
- sold-out/limited-quantity notices;
- page numbers or navigation artifacts.

Do not use a brittle exact-string blacklist as the primary solution. Combine lexical role, geometry, neighborhood and product evidence.

### P0.2 Preserve spatial provenance
OCR text must retain enough page/image geometry and source provenance for downstream reconstruction.

Every meaningful token/line/region should remain attributable to:
- source document/image;
- page when applicable;
- bounding box / coordinates where available;
- region/cell association;
- confidence/evidence needed for later decisions.

Do not flatten all recognized text into one semantic bag before product reconstruction.

### P0.3 Product-region / cell reconstruction
Reconstruct products from spatially coherent regions rather than arbitrary text fragments.

Use evidence such as:
- product image/visual cell boundaries when available;
- ITEM/SKU anchors;
- product-name anchors;
- price blocks;
- row/column alignment;
- whitespace/gutters;
- typography grouping;
- repeated catalog layout;
- local neighborhood relationships.

A real product should normally produce exactly one candidate. A product’s name, model, specification, ITEM, prices and conditions must stay within the same product region unless there is strong evidence of a document-level shared field.

### P0.4 Prevent cross-product contamination
Introduce explicit guardrails so information from product A cannot silently populate product B.

Do not merge regions solely because they share:
- brand;
- row;
- nearby price;
- similar wording;
- page-level campaign context.

Fragment merging must require positive evidence that fragments belong to the same product. Ambiguous merges must remain separate or require review rather than contaminating a candidate.

### P0.5 Merchant / brand / product separation
Keep these concepts separate:
- merchant/source: e.g. Costco / supermarket;
- brand: product manufacturer/brand when supported;
- product name: complete product identity;
- model;
- specification;
- ITEM/SKU;
- promotion conditions.

For catalog imports, document-level merchant may be inherited by products only when the document source is reliable. A product brand must never replace merchant/source merely because it is recognized confidently.

### P0.6 Price semantics
Represent price roles explicitly.

Fields may include:
- original price;
- promotional price;
- savings;
- quantity/bundle condition;
- unit/average price where explicitly labelled;
- other price text retained as evidence/notes when role is uncertain.

Rules:
- never treat a standalone number as original price without semantic evidence;
- never treat unit/average price as original price merely to fill the field;
- never treat spend threshold as product price;
- calculate savings only when original and promotional prices are reliable, compatible and refer to the same purchase condition;
- if original price is absent/uncertain, keep it null / `原價未提供` and leave calculated savings empty;
- preserve explicit bundle conditions such as 任選2件, multi-pack, member/payment restrictions.

### P0.7 Date provenance
Distinguish document-level campaign dates from product-specific dates.

A shared date may be inherited only when the layout/document clearly applies it to the product. Conflicting product-level dates override shared context. Uncertain dates must require review rather than being fabricated.

### P0.8 Final Validation Gate
Before persistence, validate critical relationships, not just field presence.

No record may be persisted automatically when critical evidence is contradictory or unsafe.

At minimum validate:
- usable product identity/name;
- valid required date behavior according to existing app rules;
- no rejected/non-product classification;
- merchant/brand role sanity;
- price relationship sanity;
- candidate not identified as a duplicate fragment of another candidate;
- required compatibility with existing coupon model/reminder behavior.

Persistence must remain atomic. A validation failure must not partially corrupt saved coupons or reminders.

## 4. P1 priorities
### P1.1 Confidence and review routing
Use at least these outcomes conceptually:
- READY: high-confidence, internally consistent candidate;
- NEEDS REVIEW: potentially useful product but one or more uncertain/required fields;
- REJECTED: not a product / unsafe candidate.

READY may be preselected only when all persistence-critical gates pass.
NEEDS REVIEW must not be preselected by default.
REJECTED should not appear as a normal importable coupon candidate.

### P1.2 Review UX to reduce work
Problems must be obvious and actionable.

Required behavior:
- missing/uncertain required information displayed prominently, using the established red problem treatment;
- all problem candidates sorted before clean candidates;
- `只看需要確認` remains available;
- problem candidates can be selected and then corrected in the focused review/edit flow where supported;
- bottom summary clearly shows problem count;
- preserve sequential focused review for unresolved candidates;
- avoid forcing users to open every clean candidate.

### P1.3 Candidate deduplication
Detect obvious duplicate fragments of the same product and retain/merge the most complete coherent evidence without merging distinct products.

### P1.4 Classification
Keep a practical finite set of categories suitable for supermarket/catalog content. Existing V0.15 category compatibility must be preserved.

Classification should use complete product evidence, not brand alone. If uncertain, use `其他` rather than inventing a category.

### P1.5 Import quality metrics and regression fixtures
Add measurable import-quality evaluation so progress is not described only as “better”.

Where ground-truth fixtures are available, support metrics such as:
- Candidate Recall;
- Candidate Precision;
- One-to-One Product Reconstruction Rate;
- non-product false-positive rate;
- duplicate candidate rate;
- critical field correctness;
- Needs Review capture rate;
- direct-import / READY rate;
- manual correction rate or proxy;
- import processing time where testable.

Do not fabricate metric values when Founder datasets have not been labelled/measured.

Add regression fixtures/tests for representative failure patterns, including multi-product supermarket layouts and multi-product catalog/PDF layouts, using synthetic/redacted fixtures if original Founder assets cannot legally or practically live in the repository.

## 5. P2 — UI polish only when necessary
UI polish is allowed only when required to support the new review workflow or prevent misunderstanding. Do not spend V0.16 on unrelated visual redesign.

## 6. Required candidate information model
Preserve/extend the existing V0.15 model as necessary to represent evidence safely. Candidate information should support, where available:
- coupon/product display name = brand + product name + model/spec as appropriate without duplicating words;
- merchant/source;
- brand;
- model;
- specification;
- ITEM/SKU;
- campaign/product date(s);
- promotional price;
- original price only when explicitly/reliably supported;
- savings only when safely derivable;
- promotion/bundle/payment/member conditions;
- category;
- notes/evidence;
- confidence/review reasons;
- source provenance.

Do not force unknown optional values merely to make the UI look complete.

## 7. Notes presentation
When information exists and the existing UI/model requires notes, present price/condition information clearly, conceptually like:

原價：○○元 (only when reliable)
優惠價：○○元
共省：○○元 (only when safely calculated)
優惠條件：任選2件／指定支付／限會員
商品規格：○○g × ○包

Do not generate misleading placeholder numeric values.

## 8. Compatibility and safety constraints
V0.16 must NOT break:
- reminders / scheduling / notification deep link;
- search;
- filtering;
- sorting;
- Dashboard;
- My Day;
- favorites;
- categories;
- batch operations;
- backup / restore;
- existing local data.

Maintain backward compatibility with supported V0.8, V0.9, V0.10, V0.11 and subsequent existing backup formats already supported by the codebase. Do not casually rename persisted identifiers.

Remain Android-first and Local-first.

No external/cloud OCR, external LLM/AI processing, account/login, cloud sync or analytics unless separately approved by Founder. If existing implementation already uses a dependency contrary to this statement, stop only if it creates a material product/privacy contradiction; report the actual code evidence rather than silently changing architecture.

## 10. Acceptance criteria
V0.16 is engineering-ready only when:
1. non-product fixture regions do not become normal candidates;
2. one-product fixtures reconstruct to one candidate rather than fragmented duplicates;
3. distinct neighboring products remain distinct;
4. merchant and brand remain semantically separate;
5. price-only text cannot become product name/source;
6. original price remains absent when not reliably supplied;
7. savings is calculated only from reliable compatible price roles;
8. shared campaign context does not overwrite conflicting product-level evidence;
9. uncertain candidates are routed to NEEDS REVIEW and are not preselected by default;
10. READY candidates satisfy the Final Validation Gate;
11. rejected content cannot be persisted as a normal coupon;
12. final write/reminder update remains atomic and safe;
13. existing app regression suite remains green;
14. new regression tests cover the V0.16 failure classes;
15. `flutter analyze` passes;
16. all automated tests pass;
17. Android Debug APK is produced by CI;
18. Draft PR remains unmerged;
19. Founder real-device acceptance remains required using representative supermarket image(s) and Costco PDF.

No claim of 100% universal recognition may be made without measured labelled evidence. The 100% gate applies to records allowed to pass persistence validation, with uncertain records withheld for review.
